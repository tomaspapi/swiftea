Privacy Policy

Swiftea is developed by Tomás Papi (the “Developer”). It is designed to control your Ember Mug without an account, a cloud service, ads, or developer-operated tracking. Mug information stays on your Mac. The Developer does not receive your mug readings, history, names, or identifiers. This policy explains what Swiftea handles locally, when it connects to the internet, and what choices you have.

Version 1.0 · Effective July 20, 2026

At a glance

Swiftea has no account or sign-in, developer-operated advertising, analytics, telemetry, cross-site tracking, or cloud upload of mug readings or history. The Developer does not sell or rent personal information. Mug history stays on your Mac for up to 30 days. Internet access is limited to software updates, the Swiftea website, and links you choose to open.

Information handled on your Mac

Swiftea uses Bluetooth to find, connect to, and control compatible mugs. To do that, the app may process device identifiers and advertised Bluetooth information; mug details such as its name, model, color, size, and serial number; and live readings such as temperature, battery, charging, contents, connection, and heating state.

Swiftea also stores preferences and app state locally, including saved mugs, mug aliases, auto-connect choices, target temperatures, display settings, notification choices, and records showing which versions of the Terms of Use and Safety Notice you accepted.

Battery and temperature history is stored in your Mac’s Application Support folder. History is separated by mug and automatically pruned after 30 days. Native notifications are created on your Mac when you enable them. Diagnostic logs are local and managed by macOS; Swiftea does not upload them, and device-specific details are treated as private by default.

The Developer cannot remotely access this local information.

Software updates

When automatic update checks are enabled, or when you check manually, Swiftea uses Sparkle to request its public update feed from GitHub Pages and may download an update from GitHub Releases. Swiftea does not send mug names, serial numbers, readings, history, or Bluetooth identifiers with these requests, and it does not opt in to Sparkle’s optional system-profile reporting.

As with an ordinary web request, GitHub may receive network information needed to deliver the feed or file, such as your IP address, the requested URL, request time, and basic request or device metadata. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Website

The Swiftea website does not contain Developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking code. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. This logging is performed by GitHub as the website host, not by a tracker added by Swiftea. The Developer does not receive mug data through the website.

If you follow a link to GitHub or another external website, that service’s own privacy practices apply. Swiftea is not responsible for third-party websites or services.

Sharing and sale

The Developer does not sell, rent, or share your mug information or app activity for advertising, profiling, or analytics. Swiftea does not have a backend that receives this information. Infrastructure providers may receive the limited network information described above when they deliver the website, update feed, or app download.

Retention and deletion

Mug history is automatically deleted after 30 days. Other locally stored preferences and saved mug details remain on your Mac until you change them, forget a mug, or delete Swiftea’s local data. Forgetting a mug removes its saved connection details and alias; any existing history for that mug continues to age out under the 30-day limit.

Deleting the app alone may not remove its local data. To erase Swiftea’s remaining local data, quit Swiftea and remove ~/Library/Application Support/Swiftea and ~/Library/Preferences/com.tom.swiftea.plist.

The Developer has no Swiftea account record or server-side mug data to access or delete.

Permissions and choices

Bluetooth access is required to discover and control a mug. Notification access is optional and can be changed in macOS System Settings. Automatic update checks can be turned off in Swiftea’s Settings.

The Swiftea website does not perform cross-site tracking, so it does not change its behavior in response to “Do Not Track” signals.

Children’s privacy

Swiftea is not directed to children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Swiftea.

Security

Keeping mug information on your Mac reduces the amount of data exposed to online services, but no software or device is perfectly secure. Keep macOS up to date and protect access to your Mac.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Swiftea’s features or privacy practices change. Material changes will be posted on the Swiftea website and reflected in the policy available inside the app. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published. If a future change requires consent under applicable law, Swiftea will request it before the affected feature is used.

Contact

Swiftea is developed by Tomás Papi. Privacy questions can be raised through the Swiftea project at https://github.com/tomaspapi/swiftea. Do not include mug serial numbers, logs, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
